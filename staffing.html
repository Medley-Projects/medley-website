How We Source

A process built for better matches

Great hires come from rigor, not volume. Our process combines deep industry understanding with technology that surfaces the best candidates — and human judgement that closes the deal.

01

Understand the Role

We dig into your team, culture, and the real requirements of the role — so we source for fit, not just a keyword match.

02

Source & AI-Screen

AI-assisted sourcing and resume parsing surface the strongest candidates, filtered by skills, experience, and fit signals.

03

Interview & Verify

Candidates are interviewed, skills-tested, and reference-checked before you ever see them — so your time is spent on top matches.

04

Place & Support

We manage onboarding and stay engaged after placement — ensuring a smooth start and long-term success for both sides.
