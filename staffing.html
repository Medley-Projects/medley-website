Our Process

How We Work

Our six-step placement process is built around quality over speed. Starting from client requisition to candidate start, every candidate passes through structured screening, skills validation, and background checks before being submitted — so you spend less time interviewing and more time building.

6 Stage Vetting Process
25% Pass Technical Interview
6% Submitted to Client
100% Background & E-Verified
1

Source Candidates

Internal DB (Ceipal), Job Fairs, Indeed, Dice, CareerBuilder, ZipRecruiter, Employee Referrals, LinkedIn

2

Pre-Screening & Screening

Resume review, skills & experience verification, discussion of past projects

3

Language & Soft-Skill Assessment

Communication, personality evaluation, English proficiency

4

Technical Vetting Interview

Validation test, professional reference check, basic background check

Only 25% advance
5

Submission to Client

Shortlisted candidates forwarded for client's own interview process

Top 6% only
6

Candidate Onboarding

Detailed background check, drug test, E-Verify — ready to start
