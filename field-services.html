What We Deliver

Field capabilities across the network

Our technicians handle the full range of network construction, installation, and maintenance work — with documented quality checkpoints at every step.

Construction

Structured Cabling & Construction

  • Structured cabling & cable priming
  • Pre-insulated & insulated copper lines
  • Fiber raceway installation
  • RF cabling & UPoCC
  • Fiber & optical jumper installation
  • A-cable assembly
  • Grounding, bonding & testing
  • Civil & structural work (duct bank, concrete, panels)
Install

Equipment Install & Swaps

  • BBU (baseband unit) swaps
  • Router migration & swaps
  • SiteBoss installation
  • Optical transport installation
  • Microwave installation
  • Mobile & fixed wireless installs
  • Seismic mounting & brackets
  • In-building work authorizations (ISP)
Testing

Testing, Maintenance & Backhaul

  • CAT6 / CAT6A cabling
  • Backhaul work orders (BHO)
  • Functional & call testing
  • Pre- & post-install verification
  • Remote PHY / HFC node work
