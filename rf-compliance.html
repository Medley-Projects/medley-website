Engagement Model

How we work with you

Whether it's a single assessment before a lease renewal or an ongoing compliance program across a portfolio, we structure our support around your deadlines and requirements.

01

Site & Scope Intake

We gather site data, carrier configurations, and the specific compliance requirement — lease, audit, remediation, or new build — so the work is scoped precisely.

02

Assessment & Analysis

RF engineers run EME calculations, contour analysis, and predictive modeling against the governing standard — FCC, OSHA, or carrier-specific requirements.

03

Documentation & Mitigation

You receive accurate, defensible reports — plus mitigation recommendations and compliant signage where needed, installed correctly the first time.

04

Audit & Renewal Support

Documentation is organized and maintained for lease audits, carrier requests, and scheduled re-assessments, so you're always ready when asked.
