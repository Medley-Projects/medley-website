Our Process

How We Work

Our RF compliance process is designed to be thorough, fast, and documentation-ready from day one. We combine certified engineering expertise with efficient field operations to deliver compliant, audit-ready outputs — whether you're managing a single site or a nationwide portfolio of hundreds.

5 Step Compliance Process
FCC Part 1 Subpart I Certified
48h Typical Report Turnaround
100% Audit-Ready Documentation
1
Site Intake & Scoping
Collect site details, antenna parameters, carrier data, and access requirements. Define deliverables and timeline.
2
EME Analysis & Calculations
MPE calculations for all transmitters, occupational and general population exposure modeling, and co-location aggregate analysis.
3
Compliance Report & Signage
Deliver FCC/OSHA-compliant report with findings, exclusion zone diagrams, and any required RF hazard signage specifications.
4
Field Survey & Measurement
On-site visit to measure RF field levels, document antenna inventory, map exclusion zones, and capture site photographs.
5
Remediation & Follow-Up
Where gaps are identified, we recommend corrective actions, support implementation, and re-assess to confirm compliance.
