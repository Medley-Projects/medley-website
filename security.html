SECURITY VULNERABILITY REPORTING


To report a suspected security vulnerability affecting Medley’s website or systems, email info@medleynetworks.com with the subject “Security Vulnerability Report.” Include the affected URL or system, a description, and safe reproduction steps. Do not include passwords or sensitive data.

We acknowledge reports within one business day, investigate, and coordinate remediation. Do not conduct unauthorized testing, disrupt services, or access others’ data.
